Many ideas feel perfectly clear when they first appear. A few days later, if they were never written down, only a vague outline remains.

This blog is not an attempt to keep up with a publishing schedule or produce a verdict on every question. It is closer to a public notebook: a place to record how I understand something, preserve the evidence and reasoning, and leave room for my future self to revise it.

> Writing is not simply copying down a finished thought. Often, the act of writing is how we discover what we have not yet understood.

## What I Want to Record

- The design choices behind technology, not only the finished code.
- Ways of working that remain useful, along with the boundaries where they fail.
- Fragments from books, journeys, and ordinary days that deserve to stay.

## One Simple Principle

I want each post to answer at least one concrete question. It does not need to be grand, but a reader should leave with a clearer judgment or a path they can test for themselves.

This is where I begin, one note at a time.
