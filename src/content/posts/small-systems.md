Many plans fail not because the goal is unimportant, but because beginning requires too much energy.

A useful small system usually has three qualities: the action is small, the feedback is quick, and recovery is easy.

## Lower the Cost of Starting

“Write an article every day” quickly becomes pressure. “Save one question worth thinking about” is much easier to begin. The first demands completion; the second makes room for accumulation.

## Make Interruption Normal

Every long-running system will be interrupted. The goal is not perfect continuity, but a design that lets you restart without a ceremony.

I want this blog to work the same way. Posts can be short, slow, and revised later, but each idea has a clear place where the next round of thinking can continue.
